# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no framework and no build step. Chosen because the site is a small lead-generation marketing site with no dynamic data, it must be easy to host anywhere (Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, or a shared host) and easy to hand off, and Impeccable live mode works directly on static HTML files. Shared header and footer are kept consistent by hand across pages; revisit this choice only if the site grows past a handful of pages.

## Users

Primary user: the owner or decision maker of a healthcare business in Brazil (clinics, medical and dental practices, individual doctors and other health professionals) looking for a marketing partner. They arrive from a referral, a social profile, or a search, usually on a phone, and want to know quickly whether RNK understands their sector and how to start a conversation.

Language and market: Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR), Brazil.

## Product Purpose

RNK is a marketing agency specialised in the healthcare and medical niche. The site exists to present RNK to prospective clients and turn interest into contact. Success is a qualified lead reaching RNK, through WhatsApp or the contact form.

## Positioning

Specialisation in healthcare marketing is the claim: RNK works with clinics and health professionals, not with every kind of business. Future copy should lean on that sector knowledge (patient journey, medical advertising rules, trust and credibility in health communication) rather than on generic agency promises.

The specific services, results, and differentiators have not been confirmed yet. Record them here before any surface makes concrete service or results claims.

## Operating Context

- Leads are handled in conversation, primarily over WhatsApp. The site is the front door, not the sales process.
- Healthcare advertising in Brazil is regulated (for example by the Conselho Federal de Medicina and the Conselho Federal de Odontologia). Client-facing examples and promises must respect those limits; the agency's own positioning benefits from showing that awareness.
- Most visits are expected on mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed:

- Lead channels on the site: WhatsApp click-to-chat and a contact form.
- Site language: pt-BR only.

Undecided (do not invent):

- The WhatsApp business number and where form submissions should be delivered (email address or a form service such as Formspree, Web3Forms, or Netlify Forms). A static site needs a third-party service or serverless endpoint for the form.
- The list of services offered and their names.
- Region served (city, state, or all of Brazil) and whether service is remote or in person.
- Pricing, packages, and any guarantees.
- Hosting and domain.

## Brand Commitments

The name "RNK" is confirmed. No logo, colours, typography, voice, or visual references have been provided or made binding. Nothing else about the identity is established.

## Evidence on Hand

None confirmed. No logo file, photos, client names, testimonials, case studies, results figures, or press have been provided. Surfaces must not fabricate any of these; use clearly labelled placeholders and ask the user for real material before launch.

## Product Principles

1. Sector credibility over agency bravado: every claim should be something a clinic owner would recognise as true about their world.
2. One job per page: get the visitor to a WhatsApp conversation or the form with as little friction as possible.
3. Truthful by construction: no invented clients, results, or reviews; placeholders are visibly placeholders.
4. Mobile first in fact, not just in layout: contact actions reachable with one thumb, fast on a cellular connection.
5. Respect the regulated context: language and examples should model what compliant healthcare marketing looks like.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement was established. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline for a public marketing site in pt-BR.
